## Action 4: First-Aid Requirements Validation (Saudi Labor Law Art. 134–135)
**Owner:** Contract & Commercial Lead | **Due:** 6 Oct 2026
**Status:** Validated

**Validation Assessment:**
- **Article 134:** Requires employers to provide medical aid for workers.
- **Article 135:** Mandates specific first-aid cabinet provisions and a dedicated medical room with a trained nurse if the worker count at a single location exceeds 50.
- **Project Application:** The peak field workforce for this SOW is estimated at 35 personnel (including multiple 2-man crews, permit receivers, and supervisors). Since the headcount per location is below 50, a fully staffed medical room is *not* legally mandated by Art. 135. However, fully stocked first-aid kits meeting Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) standards will be provided in every contractor vehicle and at the central materials yard.
